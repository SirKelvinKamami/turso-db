## Gotchas (see lessons file)
- `Box<dyn Error>` (non-Send) held across `COMMIT` await → every handler became
  non-`Handler`; fixed with `FrameError(String)` + `+ Send` boxing at the boundary.
- serde 1.0.229 `Visitor` methods take the error as a generic param (`visit_none<E>`),
  not `Self::Error` — earlier double-option visitor failed to compile.
